Systems integration.
Built around your work.

Your tools may work well individually while information gets lost between them. We review the systems you rely on, define where each piece of information belongs, and build a scoped connection that supports the way your team works.

01

Define the source of truth

Agree on which system owns contacts, appointments, requests, or status updates. Map fields and resolve conflicting formats before connecting data.

02

Confirm the connection

Review available APIs, account permissions, licensing, and technical limits. Compatibility is confirmed during discovery rather than assumed from a software logo.

03

Make the handoff dependable

Test missing fields, duplicate records, and interrupted requests. Document how the connection works and what the team should do when it needs attention.

A website inquiry becomes a CRM record

Name, company, and inquiry details map into the agreed CRM fields. Duplicate handling and missing values follow explicit rules, so staff can work from a consistent record.

A little more clarity

Questions, answered.

Can you connect our specific software?+

We first review the product, subscription tier, API access, and the workflow you need. Tell us the tools involved so we can assess feasibility.

Does integration include data cleanup?+

Data cleanup, custom APIs, and additional systems are quoted separately. We identify these needs before agreeing on the build.

What is the difference from automation?+

Integration connects systems and their data. Automation coordinates the steps in a process. Some projects need both, and the proposal makes that scope clear.
