# Dream Commerce — launch playbook (November 9, 2026)

Status: PRODUCT RESEARCH ONLY. Live store: https://shop.dreamprotocol.ai. No products have been imported into Shopify yet.

## Strategic decision

- Hero test: Astronaut Galaxy Projector. Shopify Collective candidate from Dashing Nova. Suitable for visual room-transformation videos, but the category is crowded and must prove contribution margin.
- Secondary concept: Flapping Bird Cat Toy. Real, safe demonstrations featuring an actual cat; no invented reactions or safety claims.
- Tertiary concept: Rechargeable Rolling Cat Ball. Strong video potential, but batteries and product durability make reviews/sample testing mandatory.
- Do **not** market generic dish racks, brushes, or cables as hero viral products.
- Do **not** assume a supplier-listed margin equals profit. Supplier pricing, shipping charges, payment fees, refunds, tax handling and ad spend all matter.

## First-sale gates

1. Merchant imports an authorized Shopify Collective item using its actual **Import product** control. Never replace the linked Collective product with a manually recreated item; that loses supplier sync.
2. Confirm merchant payment activation and test checkout. Confirm price, stock, US shipping zones, processing and actual carrier/tracking data.
3. Read the supplier's exact shipping service level, return eligibility, refunds, product compliance, age suitability, battery warnings and warranty responsibility. Set truthful policies on Shopify.
4. Verify SKU is connected to the supplier and inventory changes sync; place a real test order only with merchant approval.
5. Confirm all five customer paths: product > cart > checkout > supplier route > tracking > support/return.
6. Publish to buyers only when those gates pass.

## Organic creative: original demonstrations, not fabricated footage

### Galaxy projector — concept A (12–18 seconds)
Hook (0–2s): "This corner looked boring last night."
Show original footage of the ordinary room. Turn on the verified projector and film a genuine wide angle of the ceiling. Show real controls and lighting, no fake rendered nebula. On-screen text: "A different mood in seconds." CTA after product import: "See the light in Dream Commerce."

### Galaxy projector — concept B (15 seconds)
Hook: "Room makeover without repainting?"
Record two actual lighting states with the same camera exposure and the same room. Include a close-up of the actual model and label exact functions only after sample verification. CTA: verified product URL.

### Flapping bird — concept C (15–20 seconds)
Hook: "A cat's reaction is impossible to fake."
Show a cat playing voluntarily with the sample, filmed by the owner/creator with rights to the footage. No distressing the cat. Use real audio, and do not promise that every animal will love it. CTA: verified product URL.

### Rolling ball — concept D (12 seconds)
Hook: "Can an automatic toy keep up with this cat?"
Record one realistic play session on a surface the manufacturer supports. Include any actual noise and movement limitations. CTA: verified product URL.

## Publishing and growth

- Start with **organic original demos**, not paid media, because initial supplier margins are too thin to support unknown acquisition costs.
- Post approved creative across supported channels with platform-compliant disclosure, licensed music/footage and product-truth checks.
- Measure: attributed sessions, add-to-cart rate, conversion, refunded purchases, landed margin, shipping exceptions and time-to-delivery.
- Do not claim product sales/ratings or testimonials not present in verified data.
- Link each piece of content to its exact Shopify product page only once the page has been imported and verified.
- Maintain an optional business-facing "Powered by Dream Protocol" link, but do not retarget unrelated consumers as B2B leads without consent.

## Daily automation posture

A daily trend-watch task scans for new demand signals. A daily store-pulse task looks for order and fulfillment anomalies. The GitHub Actions workflow produces marketing drafts **from static candidate inputs**; it does **not** independently publish videos or create orders. No automated ad spend or supplier purchases are approved merely by this playbook.

## Minimum economics check

Example only: at a hypothetical $18.49 sale and a 50% supplier margin, gross spread is about $9.25 before platform fees, shipping adjustments, refunds, returns and acquisition cost. Zero or low paid-ad budget is the default until actual contribution profit is observed.

Go/no-go: stop a product if direct customer service/refunds or verified landed cost make the margin unacceptable. A product that draws views but loses money is not a winner.
