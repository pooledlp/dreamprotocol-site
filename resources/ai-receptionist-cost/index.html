What does an AI receptionist actually cost?

Separate setup, monthly service, usage, and integrations before comparing AI receptionist proposals.

Dream ProtocolPublished 2026-09-133 min read

THE USEFUL TAKEAWAY

Compare the total agreed scope: setup + monthly service + usage + connections + support.

Start with the conversation you need

An AI receptionist price only makes sense next to the job it needs to do. Answering approved questions and collecting callback details is a different scope from booking appointments, checking live records, or routing calls across several locations. Write down the main caller needs before comparing proposals. Decide which calls should reach a person and what information the team needs when that happens.

Separate setup from monthly service

Dream Protocol’s published front-desk starting price is $399 per month, with setup from $995 for one business/location. Setup covers business knowledge and call-flow configuration within the agreed scope. Monthly service and one-time setup should appear separately in a proposal so you can understand both the first invoice and ongoing cost.

Make usage explicit

Ask what usage allowance is included, how usage is measured, and what happens when the allowance is exceeded. Request the overage terms before launch. A business with a few brief inquiries has a different call pattern from one with frequent, detailed conversations. Use your existing call records where available rather than assuming the advertised monthly figure covers every volume.

Check connections and support

Calendar booking, CRM updates, multiple locations, or custom routing can change the project. Compatibility depends on available APIs and account access. Software licenses and third-party usage may add costs. Dream Protocol’s managed AI support starts separately at $750 per month; do not assume that plan is included in the $399 front-desk starting price.

Compare the first year using the same assumptions

Use this worksheet: setup + (12 × monthly service) + estimated usage charges + required software + separately scoped support. Leave unknown figures visible until the provider supplies them. Compare what the caller experiences, how exceptions are handled, and what your team receives—not only the monthly headline.

Your next-step checklist

  • One location or several?
  • Questions only, callback intake, or confirmed booking?
  • Included usage and overage terms?
  • Setup and integration charges?
  • Who handles updates and exceptions?
