Questions, answered.

Do you work with businesses outside technology?+

Yes. We work across industries, including local shops, professional offices, contractors, hospitality, and property teams. The task and requirements determine the project.

Do we need to replace our software?+

We start by reviewing the systems you already use. Integration depends on the APIs, permissions, and licenses available. We discuss any required changes before agreeing on scope.

Can our first step be a workshop?+

Yes. The starting workshop is a two-hour remote session for up to five people at $750, with a prompt guide and one 30-minute follow-up. Onsite delivery and larger groups are quoted separately.
