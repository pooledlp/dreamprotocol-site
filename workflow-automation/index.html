Scope and pricing

Projects start at $1,500 for one workflow across up to two existing tools, including testing, documentation, handoff, and 30-day fixes for the agreed scope.

Start with a repeated handoff

A useful first project might move a website inquiry into your CRM and notify the right person. We map the trigger, required information, owner, and next action before building. Your team decides where review or approval belongs.

Plan for incomplete information

Automation needs rules for missing fields, duplicates, and failed connections. We agree how exceptions are surfaced so your team can see what needs attention. The implementation depends on the tools and access available in your business.

Make the workflow maintainable

We test the agreed scenarios and document how the workflow runs. You receive a handoff for everyday use, with ongoing improvements available under a separately scoped support arrangement.

Questions before you start

Which tools can you connect?

We assess your existing tools and their APIs during discovery. CRM, email, forms, and calendars are common workflow building blocks; compatibility is confirmed before a proposal.

Can people approve actions?

Yes. Human review can be included at the agreed points, such as before a sensitive message is sent or a record is changed.

What changes the project price?

Additional tools, custom APIs, data cleanup, and more complex rules are quoted separately.
