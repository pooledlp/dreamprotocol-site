Workflow automation.
Built around your work.

Repeated copy-and-paste, forgotten follow-ups, and unclear ownership all point to the same opportunity: a better process. We map the trigger, required information, decisions, and next action before building the automation around your existing tools.

01

Map the work first

Identify where the request starts, what a complete record looks like, who owns it, and which decisions need review. Simplify unclear steps before automating them.

02

Connect the right steps

Move information between approved systems, organize incoming requests, create tasks, and prepare follow-ups. Confirm compatibility and permissions during discovery.

03

Plan for exceptions

Define what happens when information is missing, a connection fails, or a request falls outside the usual path. Make the owner and recovery process explicit.

Error handling is scoped with the workflow. We agree on alerts, retries where appropriate, and who handles a request that needs manual attention.
