Before you get started

Questions worth asking.

Can it work with our existing phone system?

Often, yes. We'll review your current provider, forwarding options, and routing requirements before agreeing to an integration.

Can the agent book appointments?

Appointment booking can be scoped where a compatible calendar or scheduling system provides appropriate integration and permissions. The public demo does not book appointments.

Do you support medical and dental businesses?

We offer HIPAA-compliant deployment configurations under approved agreements and safeguards. The public website scan and demo are not channels for protected health information. Compliance depends on the specific deployment and workflow.

Will this replace my staff?

The goal is to handle repetitive intake and routine calls while preserving human review, escalation, and customer service for situations that need your team.
