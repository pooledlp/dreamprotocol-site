FAQ

Buyer questions about AI front-desk systems.

What does DreamProtocol do?

DreamProtocol builds AI front-desk and workflow automation systems for small businesses. We help answer calls, qualify leads, send follow-ups, update CRMs, book appointments, and reduce repetitive admin work.

Is this just a chatbot?

No. DreamProtocol connects AI to real business workflows: calls, texts, email, CRM records, calendars, approvals, and team notifications.

Who is this for?

Local businesses that depend on fast response times: home services, med spas, clinics, hotels, legal offices, professional services, and SMB teams.

Can humans stay in control?

Yes. We can build approval steps so your team reviews important messages, updates, or actions before they are sent.

How long does a pilot take?

Most starter pilots are designed to launch in 2–4 weeks depending on integrations, call flows, and approval requirements.

What tools can you connect with?

Common systems include CRM platforms, calendars, email, Microsoft 365, Google Workspace, QuickBooks, help desk tools, phone systems, and custom workflows.

Do you only work in the Bay Area?

DreamProtocol is Bay Area-based and focuses on Bay Area and Northern California SMBs, but remote implementation is possible for the right business.
