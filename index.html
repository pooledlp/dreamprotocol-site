Buyer questions

Clear answers before you automate.

How DreamProtocol approaches AI employees, system integration, implementation, and ongoing support.

What does DreamProtocol do?

DreamProtocol builds AI employees, voice agents, workflow automations, AI-ready IT projects, and managed support. We connect calls, follow-up, CRM, calendars, inboxes, tickets, and business systems.

Is this just an AI receptionist?

No. Call handling is often the first workflow, but DreamProtocol also connects AI into SMS, email, CRM, calendars, Microsoft 365, Google Workspace, help desk workflows, approvals, and team notifications.

Can DreamProtocol work with our current systems?

Usually. We map the existing phone, CRM, calendar, inbox, accounting, ticketing, and cloud environment first. Custom API work is scoped where a supported connector is not available.

Does AI replace our staff?

The goal is to remove repetitive response and coordination work while keeping people in control. Escalation and human approval are designed around business risk and policy.

What happens after launch?

DreamProtocol can monitor, tune, document, support, and expand the workflow through Managed AI Operations instead of leaving your team with an unsupported agent.

Do you serve businesses outside the Bay Area?

Yes. DreamProtocol is Bay Area based and works with Northern California and remote businesses when the workflow and implementation model are a fit.
