A practical starting point

AI training.
Built around your work.

The useful question is not whether AI can write an email. It is whether your people know when to use it, what information to provide, and how to check the result. Our workshops start with the work your team already does and turn it into repeatable practice.

01

Start with real work

Choose tasks such as customer replies, meeting summaries, internal instructions, or first drafts. We agree on your tools and the team’s starting point before the session.

02

Build review into the habit

Practice checking facts, tone, omissions, and assumptions. Discuss which information belongs in your approved tools and when a person needs to make the decision.

03

Keep learning after the session

Leave with a prompt guide and one 30-minute follow-up. Use that conversation to discuss what worked, what felt difficult, and where the team needs a clearer process.

An illustrative workflow

A customer email becomes a useful draft

The team provides the approved background, asks for a draft, checks factual details, and adjusts the tone before sending. The exercise becomes a repeatable prompt with a review checklist.
