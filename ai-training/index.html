Scope and pricing

A team AI workshop is $750 for a two-hour remote session for up to five people, including a prompt guide and one 30-minute follow-up. Onsite delivery is quoted separately.

Use real tasks to build useful habits

A customer email, a first draft, or an internal summary gives the team something concrete to practice. We shape the session around the agreed tasks so participants can connect what they learn to everyday work.

Practice reviewing the output

Useful AI work includes checking facts, adjusting tone, and deciding what needs human judgment. We build review into the exercise and discuss what information is appropriate for the tools your team uses.

Leave with a next step

The prompt guide supports continued practice after the session. The included follow-up gives your team a chance to discuss questions from applying the workshop to real work.

Questions before you start

Do we need technical experience?

The workshop is built around business tasks. We agree on the team’s starting point and tools before the session.

Can you train a larger team?

Larger groups and onsite sessions are quoted separately.

Does training include building automations?

The workshop is a training session. Workflow builds and systems integration are separately scoped services.
