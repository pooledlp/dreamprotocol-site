Handle calls when your team is busy

An AI front desk can support overflow and after-hours intake. Start with the questions your callers actually ask: what you do, when you are open, and how to request help. Your business information and escalation rules define the conversation.

Turn a conversation into a useful handoff

Capture the caller’s name, contact details, service need, and location where relevant. We scope how that context reaches your team: a CRM entry, an email, or another agreed destination. Connections depend on your systems and available APIs.

Set boundaries before launch

We map your call flow, prepare the business knowledge, and test representative calls together. Booking, transfers, and follow-up are agreed during discovery; the demo is an illustration, not confirmation that every integration is installed.

Questions before you start

Can I try it with my own business?

Yes. Enter your business website in the homepage demo. When the website analysis succeeds, you can try a voice conversation based on the information found.

Does it replace my phone system?

We first review your phone setup and routing needs. The proposed implementation explains what connects to your existing systems and what changes are required.

Is managed AI support included?

Managed AI support is a separate service. Your front-desk proposal defines its scope, usage, and ongoing charges.
