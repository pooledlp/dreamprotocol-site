A missed call can leave a customer without a next step. An AI front desk gives callers a way to explain what they need, get approved business information, and leave a useful request for your staff. We design the conversation around your services, hours, service area, and handoff rules.

01

Business knowledge

Build from the services, hours, locations, and answers you approve. Decide which questions the agent can answer and which belong with a person.

02

03

A little more clarity

Questions, answered.

Can it book appointments?+

Booking depends on your calendar, available APIs, and scheduling rules. We confirm the integration before promising a booking flow; a callback request can be the starting point.

Can we keep our phone number?+

We review your current phone provider and routing options during scoping. Number changes, forwarding, and transfers depend on that setup.

Does it replace our staff?+

It handles the routine part of intake you choose. Your staff keep control of exceptions, professional judgment, and the final customer relationship.

Is the demo our production system?+

No. The website demo illustrates a conversation using information found on your website. A production launch includes approved knowledge, configuration, testing, and a separately agreed scope.
