## Two follow-ups, no chasing
**After 4–5 business days:** "Just following up once. The simplest pilot is one call type, one handoff path, and a real measure of missed-call capture. If this isn't a priority, no problem."

**After another week:** "Closing the loop. If call volume or front-desk workload changes, here is the walkthrough: {{specific industry URL}}. I won't keep bothering you."

Stop contacting people who opt out. Respect applicable CAN-SPAM requirements, including accurate identity, subject lines, unsubscribe handling and a valid postal address for commercial email. This kit is for manually reviewed, appropriately targeted outreach, not automated bulk delivery.

## Discovery call, 15 minutes
1. About how many inbound calls do you receive on a normal day, and when are they missed?
2. What three questions do callers ask most?
3. Which calls can be answered from approved facts, and which must reach a person?
4. What phone system do you use? What calendar, CRM or practice-management system?
5. Who should receive call summaries, and how do they act on them?
6. How would we know the pilot worked after 30 days?

**Close:** "I'd propose a simple first version with one approved intake flow and explicit escalation rules. I'll send the scope, monthly usage assumptions, setup requirements and total pricing before we start."
